## Why four

The programme has two competing goals, stated plainly in [`WHY.md`](WHY.md): finish
high-quality work now, and stay free to question everything later. Those goals fight each
other inside a single repository. So they are separated by structure rather than by
discipline — because the author's own account names spiralling into redesign as the failure
mode, and discipline is what fails first under that pressure.

| | Stability requirement | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| **EMOCD** | High — it is a Phase I deliverable | Aerospace companies, recruiters, reviewers |
| **EMOCD-paper** | Follows the flagship exactly | Researchers reproducing the work |
| **EMOCD-thesis** | Follows the flagship, plus university material | Examiners, viva panel |
| **EMOCD-lab** | **None. Instability is the point** | Me, and anyone curious about what comes next |

## Which are generated, and why that matters

**`EMOCD-paper` and `EMOCD-thesis` are generated by `tools/export_companion.py`. They are
never hand-edited.** Every file in them carries a header saying so.

This is not tidiness. The exit velocity `16.537 m/s` appears in nine places in the flagship
alone. Four hand-maintained repositories would make divergence a certainty, and this project
has already produced two forks of exactly that kind:

- The operating point forked between `analysis/motor_model.py` and `analysis/sizing.py` —
  now guarded by `_check_operating_point()`, which exits with a diagnostic if they drift.
- The paper figures forked from the analysis — now regenerated by `paper/make_figures.py`,
  which imports the analysis rather than reimplementing it.

Generation is the same answer applied a third time. If a companion is ever hand-edited, the
correct response is to delete and regenerate it, not to reconcile the two.

**If you found a mistake in a companion, fix it here.**

## Where redesign goes

`EMOCD-lab` is Phase II. Anything that would improve the design rather than correct it goes
there — the momentum-transfer release, the rib-stiffened chassis, the two-layer stator, the
envelope repackaging.

It has no baseline and makes no stability promise. That is deliberate: it is the pressure
valve that lets the flagship stay frozen. Items leave it only at baseline boundaries, against
criteria written when they were deferred — see [`docs/PHASE_II.md`](docs/PHASE_II.md).
